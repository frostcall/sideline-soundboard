The game-day soundboard for Android

Goal songs, horns and announcements on big tiles you can tap mid-game, made for whoever runs the music at youth hockey, soccer and other games.

Help test the app See what it does

Android 8 and newer. Not available for iPhone.

Every sound is one big tile

Songs, horns, sound effects and announcements each get a big colored tile, organized into pages you switch between with tabs.

Songs play from YouTube and YouTube Music links, and you can import a whole playlist at once. Audio files on your phone work too.

CUE it, then GO

Line songs up in Up Next while play is going on, then start the next one the moment the whistle blows.

Up Next shows how long each clip will play, so you know what fits before the next faceoff or kickoff.

Horns and announcements over the music

Horns and sound effects play on top of whatever song is running and start instantly. FADE and STOP are always one tap away.

For announcements, type the text and your phone's built-in voice reads it out. The music dips while it speaks.

Action tiles run a whole routine

One tap can run several steps in order. For example: play the goal horn, play an intro, wait a moment, then start a playlist.

Quick actions within reach

Keep your most-used buttons in a quick action bar. On a phone, a small-video mode fits more tiles on screen. Folding phones and tablets get the side panel layout shown at the top.

What it costs

Playing songs is free. A one-time Pro purchase unlocks extras such as custom colors and quick action buttons.

Help get it on Google Play

Google requires a closed test with real testers before a new app can be released. If you have an Android phone, you can help and get the app early.
