Help test Sideline Soundboard

Sideline Soundboard is an Android app for running the music at youth games. Before a new app can go on Google Play, Google requires a closed test with real testers. That's where you come in.

Why join

You help get it released. Google won't publish a new app until it has been through a closed test with real people using it.

You get it early. Use it at your next game or practice before it's public.

You get Pro for free. Testers get a promo code that unlocks Pro, so you can try everything without paying.

Your feedback decides what gets fixed first. The app is made for coaches, parents and volunteers who run the music at games, so what you run into is exactly what I need to hear.

What you need

  • An Android phone or tablet running Android 8 or newer.
  • A Google account. The address you send must be the one signed in to the Play Store on that device.
  • To keep the app installed for about two weeks. Try it at a game or practice if you can.

How it works

  1. Email me your Google account address. Use the button above. It's the only thing I need from you.
  2. I add you to the tester list. Then I reply with an opt-in link.
  3. Open the link I send back. It's Google's opt-in page. Tap to become a tester.
  4. Install from Google Play. The app appears in the Play Store like any other app.
  5. Use it, and reply with what you find. Anything broken, confusing or missing. Short notes are fine.

What you'll be testing

A board of big colored tiles for songs, horns and announcements, grouped into pages. Songs stream from YouTube and YouTube Music through YouTube's own player, and your own audio files work too. Horns play over the music and start instantly. You line songs up in Up Next and start them with GO. Typed announcements are read out by your phone's built-in voice while the music dips. Some newer features, such as action tiles, are still being tested, and those are exactly where your notes help most. See the full tour.

YouTube songs need a signal and stop if you leave the app. Audio files, horns and announcements work offline.

Questions

What do you do with my email?
I use it only to add you to the test and to reply to you. I don't share it or add you to any mailing list. The Privacy Policy covers what the app itself does.
Does it cost anything?
No. Joining the test is free, and playing songs in the app is free. Pro is normally a one-time purchase, but testers don't pay for it: I'll email you a promo code that unlocks Pro, and you redeem it in Google Play. See what's Free and what's Pro.
Is there an iPhone version?
Not at this time. It's Android only.
Can I leave the test?
Yes, any time. Uninstall the app, or email me and I'll take you off the list.
