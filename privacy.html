Privacy Policy

Sideline Soundboard for Android · Effective September 27, 2026

Sideline Soundboard ("the app") is made by Thomas Howard ("I", "me"). This policy explains what information the app handles. The short version: the app has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and I don't run any server that receives your data. What you set up stays on your phone.

Information stored on your phone

The app stores the following only on your device, in the app's private storage:

  • Your boards: pages, tiles, song and sound names, tags, colors, symbols, clip start and end points, quick actions, profiles and settings.
  • Audio files you choose to add. The app copies them into its own storage so they keep working if the originals move.
  • Which songs have played during the current game, and song titles and lengths looked up from YouTube (see below).

I never receive any of this. It is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its storage in Android settings.

Backups

If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include the app's board and settings files (not your audio files) in your device backup, which Google stores for you under Google's Privacy Policy. You can turn this off in your phone's backup settings. Backup files you create yourself with "Back up everything" are saved wherever you choose and are not sent to me.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. When you add a YouTube or YouTube Music song or playlist, the app sends the song or playlist ID to YouTube to get titles, lengths and whether a video can be played in other apps. Songs play in YouTube's embedded player, which loads directly from YouTube. YouTube and Google receive information from these requests and from the player (such as your IP address, device details and cookies) and handle it under the Google Privacy Policy. By using the app you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service.

The app does not sign in to your YouTube or Google account and does not access any private account data. Song titles and lengths received from YouTube are stored on your phone and refreshed or deleted within 30 days. If you have ever connected a Google account to an app through YouTube, you can review and remove that access at Google's security settings; Sideline Soundboard does not request such access.

Other services on your phone

  • Text-to-speech announcements use the text-to-speech engine installed on your phone (for example Google's). Some engines may process text online under their own privacy policy.
  • Purchases, if you buy the paid upgrade, are handled by Google Play. I receive no payment card details; the app only checks with Google Play whether the upgrade was purchased.
  • Voice recordings: if you record a voiceover, the app uses your phone's microphone only while you're recording, and the recording is saved in the app's storage on your phone like your other audio files. It is never sent to me or anyone else.
  • Crash reports: if the app crashes, it saves technical details (app and Android version, phone model and where the error happened, nothing about your songs or boards) on your phone and shows them the next time you open it. They are sent only if you choose "Send report", by email from your own email app.
  • Sharing: when you share a link or audio file to the app from another app, the app receives only what you share.

Children

The app is intended for adults running audio at sports events. It is not directed to children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. If the change matters, the app will ask you to review and agree again.
